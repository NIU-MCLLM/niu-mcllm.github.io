Submit an abstract

Abstracts are due Friday, January 15, 2027. You'll get an email copy of your submission right away.

Before you start

  • 250–300 words, including title, author(s), affiliation(s), and contact information
  • Presentations run 15 minutes maximum
  • One submission per person; the committee assembles panels
  • In person, in English, no registration fee
  • Decisions are sent 3–4 weeks after the deadline
