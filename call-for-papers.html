35th Annual MCLLM · April 3–4, 2027

Call for Papers: Sustenance

Holmes Student Center, Northern Illinois University, DeKalb, Illinois · Keynote: Dr. Anna Kornbluh, University of Illinois Chicago

The Department of English at Northern Illinois University invites proposals for the 35th annual Midwestern Conference on Literature, Language, and Media. This year's theme is Sustenance.

Sustenance is what keeps us going. It is food and the labor, land, and rituals that bring it to the table. It is also the less visible nourishment of language, memory, faith, art, and community. Writers, speakers, and media makers have long asked who is fed and who goes hungry, what keeps a language or tradition alive, and which stories carry people through scarcity, displacement, and change.

We welcome papers that take up sustenance in its literal and figurative senses, across periods, genres, languages, and media. Possible topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Food, hunger, and foodways in literature and film
  • Scarcity and abundance; famine, rationing, and plenty
  • Land, agriculture, labor, and the environment
  • Language maintenance, revitalization, and loss
  • Heritage, memory, and the traditions that sustain communities
  • Care, kinship, and mutual aid
  • Spiritual, emotional, and creative nourishment
  • Sustainability and climate in narrative and media
  • Media ecosystems: what keeps stories, archives, and audiences alive
  • Translation as a means of keeping texts alive
  • Teaching and learning as sustenance; sustaining the humanities

MCLLM also welcomes papers on any aspect of literature, language, linguistics, film, and media studies, as well as creative work and pedagogy. Graduate students are especially encouraged to submit, including first-time presenters.

How to submit

  • Submit an abstract of 250–300 words for a presentation of no more than 15 minutes.
  • Include your title, author(s), affiliation(s), and contact information.
  • One submission per person. The committee will group accepted papers into panels.
  • The conference is in person, in English, and free to attend.
  • Abstracts are due Friday, January 15, 2027. Decisions will be sent 3–4 weeks after the deadline.

Questions? Contact mcllm@niu.edu.
