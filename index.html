2027 Theme

Sustenance

What sustains us? MCLLM 2027 explores sustenance in literature, language, and media: what nourishes bodies, communities, languages, and stories, and what happens when it runs short.

About the conference

For more than three decades, MCLLM has brought together graduate students and scholars working across literature, language, linguistics, film, and media. Hosted by the Department of English at Northern Illinois University, it is a welcoming, free conference for sharing new work, including for first-time presenters.

Keynote: Dr. Anna Kornbluh, University of Illinois Chicago
