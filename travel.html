Travel & Venue

Holmes Student Center, Northern Illinois University · 340 Carroll Avenue, DeKalb, IL 60115

The venue

All sessions take place in the Holmes Student Center on Northern Illinois University's DeKalb campus. Room assignments will be listed in the program.

Getting here

DeKalb is about 65 miles west of downtown Chicago, near Interstate 88. Chicago O'Hare and Chicago Rockford International are the closest airports. See NIU's campus maps and directions.

Parking

NIU's pay parking hours are Monday through Friday, 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. For visitor lots near the Holmes Student Center, see directions and parking, or call NIU Parking Services at 815-753-1045.

Where to stay

The Hotel at Holmes is inside the Holmes Student Center, steps from the conference rooms. More hotel suggestions will be posted here.

Accessibility

If you need accommodations to attend or present, email mcllm@niu.edu and we'll help arrange them.
